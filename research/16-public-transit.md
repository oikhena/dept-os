The crisis is acute and worsening. Predictive attrition, AI recruiting, fatigue-aware scheduling are wide open. |
| **3** | **AI for State DOT Highway Operations** | VERY LARGE | LOW | ~$51B traffic management segment | Flow Labs has ~$2M and 11 people. Only startup in the space. 50 state DOTs + hundreds of county/city DOTs need AI. Revenue forecasting, asset management, winter ops all open. |

### Key Takeaway

The single largest gap-to-competition ratio is in **transit operations center AI/automation** and **workforce crisis management tools**. These two areas affect every single transit agency, represent billions in operational spending, and have essentially zero AI-native startups building solutions. The driver shortage alone (174,000 projected shortfall, 90%+ turnover) is an existential crisis for the industry with no technology company directly addressing it. Meanwhile, areas like route optimization and autonomous vehicles are crowded with well-funded competitors.
