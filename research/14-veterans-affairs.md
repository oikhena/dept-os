# AI & AGENTIC AI IN VETERANS AFFAIRS & MILITARY HEALTHCARE: COMPREHENSIVE MARKET INTELLIGENCE REPORT

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The VA is the largest integrated healthcare system in the United States, serving 9.1 million enrolled veterans with a total FY2025 budget exceeding $400 billion (rising to $441.3 billion requested for FY2026). The Military Health System (TRICARE/DHA) adds another $64 billion serving 9.4 million beneficiaries. Together, this represents a $500+ billion annual addressable ecosystem undergoing simultaneous crises in staffing, IT modernization, claims processing, and care access -- creating massive opportunities for AI and agentic AI solutions.

The VA has 367 documented AI use cases (FY2025 inventory), plans to launch 10 more into production in FY2026, and has introduced an accelerated 60-day Authority to Operate (ATO) process for AI products. Congressional support is strong, with legislation like H.R. 3854 explicitly mandating AI automation for claims processing.

## 3. VA APPOINTMENT SCHEDULING & WAIT TIMES

### Market Size
- VA schedules **tens of millions** of appointments annually across 170+ medical centers
- Community care referrals growing to **$34 billion** in FY2026 (each referral involves scheduling)
- 1,500 schedulers lost in FY2025 staff cuts

### Key Pain Points
- Schedulers must "navigate a complex system environment requiring multiple applications for a single appointment" (GAO-25-106851)
- ~1 million of 2.1 million calls at certain facilities were not tracked (OIG, Feb 2026)
- Average outpatient surgery wait: **41 days** (13 days over VA target)
- Mental health new patient waits: **19 days**; established patients: **under 6 days**
- Veterans driving to facilities to make appointments because they cannot reach anyone by phone
- VA failed to meet its own standard of answering 80% of calls within 30 seconds
- Integrated Scheduling Solution (ISS) v1.0 rollout delayed; GAO found schedule not comprehensive

### Existing Companies
- **Oracle Health** -- expected to include scheduling in EHR but plagued with problems
- **No dedicated AI scheduling startup** has secured a VA contract
- **ISS** -- VA's internal modernization effort (behind schedule)

### Gaps -- MAJOR OPPORTUNITY
- **No AI-powered intelligent scheduling system** in production at VA
- **No AI call center/virtual assistant** for appointment booking
- **No predictive no-show management** tool deployed at scale
- **No AI for community care referral scheduling** optimization
- **No agentic AI receptionist** handling the millions of unanswered calls

### Competitive Landscape Density: **VERY LOW** -- this is one of the least addressed areas

### Workforce: 1,500 schedulers lost; 94% of facilities report physician shortages limiting availability

## RANKED SUMMARY OF TOP OPPORTUNITIES

Based on market size, pain point severity, competitive density, budget availability, regulatory feasibility, and timing:

### TIER 1 -- HIGHEST OPPORTUNITY (Large market, acute pain, low competition)

**1. AI-Powered VA Claims Processing & Adjudication**
- Market: $220B+ in annual benefits; 3M+ claims/year
- Pain: Record volumes, staff cuts, 85-day average processing time
- Competition: LOW (Booz Allen dominates; no AI-native startups)
- Catalyst: H.R. 3854 mandates AI automation; VA has 60-day accelerated ATO
- Why now: PACT Act creating exponential demand growth; 1,800+ evaluators lost

**2. AI Scheduling, Call Center & Access Management**
- Market: Tens of millions of appointments; $34B community care referrals
- Pain: ~1M untracked calls; 41-day surgery waits; 1,500 schedulers lost; veterans driving to facilities because phones go unanswered
- Competition: VERY LOW (no AI startup has this contract)
- Catalyst: GAO mandated improvements; ISS modernization failing
- Why now: Staff cuts make manual scheduling unsustainable

**3. AI for VA Workforce Force-Multiplication**
- Market: 455,000 VA FTEs; $171B medical care budget
- Pain: 28,000 employees lost; 4,434 severe shortages; 94% physician shortage rate
- Competition: LOW for VA-specific solutions
- Catalyst: Staffing crisis creating existential pressure to adopt AI
- Why now: VA GPT already has 100K users saving 2-3 hrs/week -- appetite is proven

### TIER 2 -- STRONG OPPORTUNITY (Significant market, clear pain, moderate competition)

**4. Veterans Mental Health & Suicide Prevention AI**
- Market: $700M dedicated congressional funding; 6,000+ annual suicides
- Pain: 1,000+ mental health providers lost; 57% of facilities short on psychologists
- Competition: LOW commercially (mostly internal VA tools)
- Regulatory challenge: Human-in-the-loop mandatory; extremely sensitive
- Why now: Congressional funding earmarked; DARPA investing

**5. AI for Community Care Network Management**
- Market: Growing from $22.5B to $34B (FY2025-2026); $1 trillion in planned private care over decade
- Pain: 40%+ of veterans using community care with minimal quality/cost oversight
- Competition: VERY LOW
- Catalyst: VA planning "Community Care Network Next Generation" massive new contract
- Why now: Spending doubling with no AI oversight tools

**6. Telehealth & Remote Patient Monitoring AI**
- Market: $8.4B AI RPM market by 2030; 2.1M VA telehealth veterans
- Pain: Only 1.5% of enrolled veterans on RPM; proven 41% hospital admission reduction
- Competition: MEDIUM (general RPM market) but LOW for VA-specific
- Why now: VA actively initiating RPM expansion projects in 2025

### TIER 3 -- EMERGING OPPORTUNITY (Smaller immediate market, growing need)

**7. GI Bill & Education Benefits AI**
- Market: $43B over 5 years; 16M claims processed
- Pain: $2.3B cost overrun; 75,000 beneficiaries hit by tech glitch
- Competition: VERY LOW (Accenture is sole incumbent)
- Risk: VA IT project track record is extremely poor

**8. Military Transition Services AI**
- Market: 200K annual transitioners; multi-billion dollar ecosystem
- Pain: Benefits navigation complexity; skill translation barriers
- Competition: LOW (mostly nonprofits)
- Revenue model challenge: Harder to monetize directly

**9. VA Supply Chain & Pharmacy AI**
- Market: 120M prescriptions/year; embedded in $171B medical budget
- Pain: Aging CMOP system; drug shortages; manual inventory
- Competition: VERY LOW
- Data challenge: VA pharmacy data access is limited

**10. TRICARE / Military Health System AI**
- Market: $64B MHS budget; 9.4M beneficiaries; $136B TriWest max contract
- Pain: Legacy claims processing; MTF capacity constraints
- Competition: VERY LOW for AI; HIGH barrier (mega-contractor dominated)
- Challenge: DOD procurement even more complex than VA

**11. EHR Migration & Interoperability AI**
- Market: $3.5B EHRM budget in FY2026; $37-50B lifecycle
- Pain: 94% of facilities still on VistA; 58% say new system increases safety risk
- Competition: LOW for AI overlays
- Risk: Oracle monopoly; political uncertainty about EHR future

## KEY CROSS-CUTTING THEMES

1. **The staffing crisis is the #1 catalyst**: With 28,000 employees lost, AI is no longer optional -- it is existential for VA operations. Every solution that force-multiplies existing staff has immediate demand.

2. **VA's 60-day accelerated ATO is a game-changer**: The traditional 12-18 month FedRAMP process was the #1 barrier for startups. The new process dramatically lowers entry barriers.

3. **Community care is the hidden goldmine**: The $22.5B-to-$34B growth in private-sector VA care with the $1 trillion 10-year plan creates enormous need for AI-powered network management, quality oversight, and cost control that almost no one is building.

4. **Legislative tailwinds are strong**: H.R. 3854 mandating AI claims processing, $700M in suicide prevention funding, and bipartisan support for VA AI adoption create policy certainty rare in government tech.

5. **VA is startup-friendly and getting more so**: The Veterans Health Venture Studio, AI Tech Sprint, and 60-day ATO show a deliberate effort to work with smaller companies, not just mega-contractors.

6. **The total addressable government market exceeds $500 billion annually** when combining VA ($441B) and MHS ($64B), with IT-specific budgets of $7.5B (VA) and meaningful portions of the DHP.
