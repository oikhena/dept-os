## 3. AI IEP / Special Education Documentation Automation

**Market Size & Cost Data:**

- Districts spent $38.8 billion on special education in FY20, averaging $13,127 per special education student

**Key Pain Points (with dollar figures):**
- Special educators spend 3+ hours per IEP on documentation (Streamline claims reduction to 10 minutes)
- Teams spend 120+ hours annually on compliance reports like LCAPs that "restart from scratch every year" (Edapt)
- Compliance violations trigger costly due-process hearings ($10K-$100K+ per case)
- The last nationwide study of special education spending was 25 years ago -- a new study begins in 2025-26 with results in 2028

**Key Players & Funding:**
- **Streamline (by Better Speech)** -- AI special education operating system, claims 90% paperwork reduction, 40% therapy time increase. Funding details not publicly disclosed.
- **Playground IEP** -- AI IEP Goal Writer, BIPs, Progress Monitoring, Scheduling. Built on no-code (Bubble). Bootstrapped/early stage. Funding details undisclosed.
- **AbleSpace** -- Goal tracking/data collection for special ed teachers. India-based, founded 2021. Minimal disclosed funding.
- **Panorama Education (Solara)** -- $115M total raised, 2,000+ school systems, 15M students. Acquired Class Companion (April 2025). AI platform with special ed guardrails.
- **Edapt** -- Compliance Composer for LCAPs, DIPs, SSIPs. Fundable through federal allocations. Early stage.

**Competitive Landscape Density:** LOW -- This is a massively underserved segment relative to the dollar magnitude of the problem. Most IEP platforms are legacy (Frontline/SEIS, GoalBook) with minimal AI. New AI-native entrants are small and early-stage.

**Gaps (Major Opportunity Area):**
- No dominant AI-native IEP platform exists at scale
- Automated compliance monitoring to prevent due-process violations is barely addressed
- Multi-language IEP translation (critical for equity) is almost nonexistent in current tools
- Integration between IEP systems and general SIS/LMS platforms is poor
- Real-time progress monitoring with AI-driven goal adjustment is nascent
- The sheer size of the problem ($38.8B in spending, 7.5M students) vs. the tiny number of AI startups targeting it represents one of the biggest gaps in all of K-12 edtech

## Summary: Where the Biggest Gaps Are

Ranked by opportunity size (market pain vs. competitive density):

1. **Special Education / IEP Automation** -- $38.8B in annual spending, 7.5M students, almost no AI-native platforms at scale. The single largest gap.
2. **District Compliance & Reporting Automation** -- 120+ hours/year per team on reports, very few startups, massive regulatory complexity creating a moat.
3. **School Facilities Management AI** -- Part of a $28B+ operations budget, virtually no AI-native K-12 startups.
4. **Counselor Workflow & Intervention Management** -- 1:385 counselor ratio crisis, early warning exists but intervention automation does not.
5. **Special Education Transportation Routing** -- Uniquely complex subset of the $28B transportation market with no specialized AI solution.

The most crowded spaces are lesson planning/content generation and basic grading automation, where dozens of well-funded startups and big tech players are already competing aggressively.
