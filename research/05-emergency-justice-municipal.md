### A. AI in 911 Dispatch Automation

**Market Size:**
- Computer Aided Dispatch (CAD) market: **$2.4-3.9B (2024)**, projected to reach **$4.9-23B** by 2030-2037, depending on source. Consensus CAGR of ~11-14%.
- Software dominates at 62.78% of revenue; cloud deployment growing at 16% CAGR.
- North America holds 42% market share.

**Workforce Crisis (the core pain point):**
- ~6,100 emergency call centers in the U.S. handling **240 million calls/year** (656,000/day)
- ~100,000 dispatchers nationwide; ~10,000 hired annually
- **25% average vacancy rate** (2019-2022); 82% of centers report being understaffed
- 166 centers report 30-49% vacancy; 92 report 50-69% vacancy; 13 report 70%+ vacancy
- 70.7% of centers rely on **mandatory overtime** to maintain coverage
- Annual turnover: 14-17%, much higher in first year
- Root causes: classified as "clerical workers" (lower pay/benefits), stressful conditions, poor work-life balance
- **Dollar impact**: Oakland spent **$2.6M in overtime** in one year; Berkeley spends **~$1M/year in overtime**; these numbers scale nationally to hundreds of millions

**Key Startups & Funding:**

| Company | Funding | Status | Notes |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|
| **Prepared** (now Axon) | $130M+ total; acquired for **$640M** | Acquired Oct 2025 | 1,000+ agencies, 49 states, 100M people covered. Backed by a16z, First Round, General Catalyst |
| **Carbyne** | Acquired by Axon for **$625M** | Acquired Q1 2026 | Next-gen call management; combined with Prepared to form "Axon 911" |
| **Aurelian** | **$14M Series A** (NEA led) | Active | YC-backed; automates 74% of non-emergency calls; serves ~5M Americans; saves dispatchers 3 hrs/day |
| **Hyper** | **$6.3M seed** (Eniac Ventures) | Active | AI voice for 911 calls; emerged from stealth 2025 |
| **Mark43** | ~$233M total funding | Active | Cloud-native public safety platform |

**Competitive Landscape:** **Moderate density, consolidating.** Axon is aggressively consolidating the space ($1.27B in acquisitions). A few well-funded startups remain (Aurelian, Hyper). Legacy incumbents (Motorola Solutions, Hexagon, Harris/L3Harris) dominate the broader CAD market. The non-emergency call triage niche has the most startup activity.

**Gaps where no one is building:**
- **Dispatcher training simulation platforms** using AI (very limited offerings)
- **Cross-agency interoperability middleware** (most CAD systems are siloed)
- **Rural/small PSAP-specific solutions** (most startups target large metro centers)
- **Dispatcher wellness/burnout monitoring** using AI
