## Top Opportunities (ranked by gap size vs competition)

1. **Water & Infrastructure** — $60B/yr problem, almost no AI competition, full sensor fit
2. **Special Education / IEP** — $38.8B/yr, 7.5M students, almost no AI-native platforms
3. **Community Health Centers (FQHCs)** — 32.5M Americans served, no purpose-built AI OS
4. **Electric Grid (small/mid utilities)** — $150B/yr outage costs, enterprise tools price out smaller players
5. **Public Defenders** — Constitutional crisis, 44% of work automatable, near-zero AI tools
6. **Vegetation Management** — $8-9B/yr utility spend, 92% of outages from vegetation
7. **Corrections Operations** — $87B+ market, $437M federal overtime, few building here
8. **Government-Side Permitting** — Only Govstream.ai ($4.9M) building for municipal reviewers

## Cross-Cutting Theme

Every vertical shares the same crisis: **aging workforce + undocumented institutional knowledge + high-stakes operations**. The meta-opportunity is capturing that knowledge before it walks out the door.
