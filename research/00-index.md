## Cross-Cutting Theme

Every vertical shares the same crisis: **aging/shrinking workforce + undocumented institutional knowledge + high-stakes operations + legacy IT systems**. The meta-opportunity is capturing operational knowledge before it walks out the door and deploying AI agents that let understaffed institutions continue functioning.

Total annual spending across all 15 completed verticals: **>$3 trillion** with <$50B in AI-native solutions addressing them.
